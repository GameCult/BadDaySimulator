The pain box

A virtual human puts a hand in the box and tries to keep it there. Nerves carry the signal, pain builds, the will pays for holding it down. Open the box, watch the readout, and decide whether to switch it off before the will breaks.

Settings

Changing a setting restarts the run. The same seed and settings always play out identically.

What this is. A deterministic toy model: one person, one hand, one stimulus. Every number is a placeholder, not physiology, and break times are properties of the model, not predictions about people. There is no body, physics, shock or impairment yet. The model is a TypeScript port of the C# reference in src/BadDay.Core, held to its output by parity tests.
